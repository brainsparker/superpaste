# SuperPaste 1.1

- A friendlier status bubble shows exactly what SuperPaste is doing without fake progress.
- Updates now download, verify, install, and relaunch from inside the app.
- The main window and settings are simpler, warmer, and easier to scan.
- Errors now offer useful next steps such as retrying or opening Settings.
- Use your own Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or custom endpoint key from Settings.
- First launch offers to move SuperPaste into your Applications folder for you.
- After granting Screen Recording, SuperPaste reopens itself — no hunting for it.
- The Settings button in the main window opens Settings again.
- Help links and support email now point to the right places.
